Services Agreement

This agreement is made between Northwind Studio ("the Studio") and Basecamp Labs ("the Client").

1. Scope. The Studio will design and deliver a marketing site of five pages, with copy supplied by the Client.

2. Fees. The Client will pay a fixed fee of 12,400 USD, half on signing and half on delivery.

3. Schedule. Delivery is due eight weeks after signing. Delays caused by late copy extend the schedule day for day.

4. Ownership. On final payment, all rights in the delivered work pass to the Client.

5. Termination. Either party may end this agreement with fourteen days' written notice.
